Step Response Methodology

The step response curve models the closed-loop transfer function between pilot stick commands and the measured gyro response. A balanced response reaches 1.0 in 35–45ms on a 5-inch quad with approximately 5%–8% overshoot and minimal ringing (damping factor around 0.72). Excessive overshoot indicates an underdamped axis requiring increased D-gain or reduced Feedforward. Sluggish rise times signify an overdamped response where P-gain or Master Multiplier can be safely increased.

Spectral Band Analysis & Filter Tuning
0 – 60 Hz: Flight Dynamics

Pilot stick commands and intentional motion. Energy here represents flight control authority.

60 – 200 Hz: Frame Resonance

Carbon fiber flex or loose hardware. Targeted by Betaflight Dynamic Notch filters.

> 200 Hz: Motor RPM Harmonics

High-frequency motor noise amplified by D-term. Suppressed with Bidirectional DShot RPM filtering.
